How to play
  • Buy invests 25% of your cash in stock. Sell sells 25% of your shares.
  • A wild bull occasionally runs through — press SPACE when it's in range to lasso it for a cash bonus.
  • Watch out for the bear — lasso it in time or it'll spook your position and cost you cash.
  • Survive 60 days. Your final score is your total portfolio value.
